# Rezlee Household OS roadmap

## Product thesis
Rezlee should be useful before a single provider is connected.
Automatic bill retrieval is an accelerator, not the prerequisite for value.

The product promise:
**Know what your place needs, run it with the people you live with, and ask Rezlee when you are unsure.**

Primary product loop:
**Ask → See today → Do → Capture → Share**

## Primary navigation
- Home: today, attention, household activity and insight
- Bills: personal + shared household money
- Ask: central Rezlee AI entry point
- Care: maintenance, chores, cleaning routines and repairs
- Vault: records, appliances, warranties and renter evidence

Settings/Profile is secondary navigation from Home, not a primary tab.

## Engagement principle
Do not optimize for artificial DAU. Optimize for recurring household utility.
A healthy Rezlee household should have a reason to return whenever something needs doing, buying, paying, remembering or figuring out.

## Build order

### 1. Household Today
- Top household actions, max three
- Cleaning/maintenance due today
- Upcoming household costs and renewals
- Shared items needing someone to act
- Rezlee insights only when they are genuinely useful

### 2. Household people + responsibilities
- Invite partner, family member or roommate
- Assign tasks
- Rotate recurring chores automatically
- Show contribution/fairness without making a leaderboard mandatory
- Notifications remove the need for one person to nag everyone

### 3. Shared shopping + supplies
- One household list
- Fast add and recurring staples
- Urgent / running-low state
- Claim who is picking something up
- Receipt scan can clear purchased items later

### 4. Shared household money
- Expense capture and receipt scan
- Equal, percentage, share and custom splits
- Recurring rent/utilities
- Household balance and settle-up
- Keep this focused on shared-home money, not personal budgeting

### 5. Renter protection
- Lease and rent reminders
- Move-in photo/evidence pack
- Security-deposit checklist
- Landlord / maintenance issue log
- Move-out checklist and exportable record

### 6. Ask / Show Rezlee
- Ask household questions in natural language
- Show a bill, receipt, appliance, warranty or repair issue
- Classify the input and send it to the right Rezlee workflow
- Explain anomalies and recommend next actions

### 7. Event-driven intelligence
- Utility/cost anomalies
- Warranty/document expiries
- Seasonal and weather-related prep
- Future replacement / maintenance outlook
- Weekly home brief and useful notifications

## Do not build early
- Generic household chat; people already have messaging apps
- Heavy personal budgeting; this is household operations
- Mandatory points/leaderboards; fairness and reduced mental load come first
- Permanent marketing tiles on Home
- Features that require manual setup before providing any value
